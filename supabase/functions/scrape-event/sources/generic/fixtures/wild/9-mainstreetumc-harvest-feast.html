Harvest Feast – Nov. 6

  • November 6, 2022
  • 5:30 pm – 7:30 pm

We had such a good time at the churchwide picnic in August—let’s get together again for food and fellowship! The Member Care Committee is sponsoring a “Harvest Feast” at 5:30pm on November 6 in the Fellowship Hall.

This will be a true covered-dish dinner so bring your most delicious entrees, sides, and desserts to share and we can gather together as a church family since we are always
BETTER TOGETHER!
