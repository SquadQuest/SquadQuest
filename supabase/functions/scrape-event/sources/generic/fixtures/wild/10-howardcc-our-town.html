Our Town
by Thornton Wilder
Directed by Bill Gillett

Step into Grovers Corners and follow George and Emily as they grow up and fall in love.  Through the town’s life we discover just how fleeting—and precious—each moment can be.  Simple, moving, and deeply human, Our Town will stay with you long after the final moment.

Plan your visit

Know Before You Go

For more detailed information please visit our Plan Your Visit webpage.

Is seating assigned or general admission?

Seating for this performance is general admission. We recommend patrons arrive 30 - 40 minutes prior to the scheduled showtime. This will allow ample time to park, purchase and/or pick up tickets, and find seating within the venue.

This is my first time at the theatre. What should I expect?

Attending a theatre or dance performance will be a positive experience for everyone if you observe a few simple courtesies. For more information, visit our Theatre Etiquette page.

Can I refund or exchange my ticket?

All sales are final and tickets cannot be refunded. Tickets for an upcoming performance may be exchanged in advance for another performance of the same event. For more information please visit our Exchange Information webpage.

ONLINE TICKET ORDERS

The Horowitz Center Box Office uses OvationTix as the online ticketing solution.  In order to purchase tickets online, patrons are required to create an OvationTix username and password prior to purchase. However, no username or password is needed to view event availability. OvationTix allows patrons to purchase single tickets to upcoming events in real time. In addition, patron receive their seats on a best available basis up to three hours prior to show time. 

PLEASE NOTE: Each online ticket order includes a $3 per ticket convenience fee. Patrons may purchase tickets in person or by phone to avoid this convenience fee.

CONFIRMATION INFORMATION

PLEASE NOTE: After submitting payment, the confirmation email receipt will be sent from customerservice@ovationtix.com with the subject, "Horowitz Center Box Office: Order Confirmation". Save or print your confirmation email for future reference. If you don't see your confirmation email within a few hours, check your spam/junk or promotions folder especially if you use Yahoo, Gmail, AOL, or Hotmail. If you don't receive it within 24 hours, email boxoffice@howardcc.edu.

EMAIL DELIVERY: In order to ensure delivery of your viewing information, please double check that the spelling of your email address is correct.
