Before you tap Install (iPhone)

  1. Open this page in regular Safari on the iPhone itself. Links opened from Slack, Mail, or other apps often open in an in-app or Private Browsing tab, where Install silently does nothing — if so, paste this page’s URL into Safari directly. (The link also does nothing on a Mac.)
  2. Your device must be registered in the provisioning profile. If install fails with “unable to install,” your UDID hasn’t been added yet — ask Chris to register it.

After it installs

  1. The icon appears on your home screen. First launch may say Developer Mode is required.
  2. Enable it: Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom to Developer Mode, turn it On. The phone restarts and asks you to confirm after reboot.
  3. Now open the app.
