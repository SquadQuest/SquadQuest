2026 FunFest: A Night at the Museum

Friday, November 13 | 4:30 PM – 8:00 PM

Buy Tickets & Sponsorships

Calling all curious kiddos, playful parents, and thrill-seeking families, FunFest: A Night at the Museum is your golden ticket to an after-hours adventure where the museum turns into your playground! Explore mind-blowing exhibits, gobble up delicious treats, and dive into fantastically fun activities that spark the imagination. Grown-ups can unwind with a cocktail in hand while the kids engage in play zones thoughtfully designed exclusively for FunFest—or take part themselves!

More Than Just a Night at the Museum

FunFest: A Night at the Museum is more than an evening of science, fun, and celebration—it's a meaningful opportunity to support the mission of The Franklin Institute.

As a fundraiser and “friend-raiser”, FunFest helps TFI:

  • Connect with families and parents of young children, building relationships that grow with their curiosity.
  • Foster long-term engagement with The Franklin Institute by welcoming the next generation of science lovers into our community.
  • Inspire future champions and supporters who will help ensure science education remains exciting, accessible, and impactful for years to come.

By attending FunFest, you're not only creating joyful memories with your family— you're also investing in the future of science learning and helping us cultivate a thriving, inclusive community of discovery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy tickets at the door?

Yes, tickets will be available for day-of purchase at the registration desk. 

Do I need a ticket to enter?

No, no physical tickets are required to enter. Upon arrival, all guests may check in under their last name at the registration desk.

Are babies free?

Yes, children 2 and under are free! Children 3+ require a ticket for entry.

Where can I park?

The Franklin Institute garage will be open for complimentary self-parking. The garage is located at 271 N. 21st Street.

Please note: All parking tickets distributed after 4:30pm at the garage entrance will be automatically marked for complimentary parking. No stickers or other validation necessary!

Will there be coat check?

Yes, coat check will be provided and located near the check-in tables in the Atrium.

Will there be food?

YES! FunFest will have plenty of food located throughout the museum. The food selection will include kid-friendly bites and small-plate dinner stations, as well as more elevated options for adults. Adults will also be able to enjoy an open bar featuring specialty cocktails.

Can I bring a stroller?

Yes, strollers are welcome! Stroller check space is limited, but you are welcome to keep your stroller with you throughout the museum. 

What should I wear?

We recommend dressing casually, including comfortable shoes!

What if my guests have changed, dropped out, or I can't make it?

No problem! Please contact us at 215-448-2378 or rsvp@fi.edu with any changes.

Does the museum have resources for guests with sensory processing needs?

Yes. Our accessibility page has more information about resources and accommodations offered throughout the Museum, including sensory backpacks. Please contact us at 215-448-2378 or rsvp@fi.edu to request any accommodations needed.

What will FunFest proceeds support?

By attending FunFest, you're not only creating joyful memories with your family— you're also investing in the future of science learning and helping us cultivate a thriving, inclusive community of discovery. Funds raised will support TFI education programs as well as discounted and free tickets for the more than 160,000 students and teachers visiting TFI every year.

If you have any additional questions, please contact Casey Satell at csatell@fi.edu or 215-448-2378.
