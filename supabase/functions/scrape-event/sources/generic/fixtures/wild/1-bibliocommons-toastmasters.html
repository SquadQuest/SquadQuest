      Public Speaking Practice With the WAO Toastmasters Club

      Event description

      Join us in person as we prepare to improve your public speaking skills, build your leadership skills, maximize your potential and more with the Wrightwood-Ashburn Overcomers (WAO) Toastmasters Club, in partnership with the Wrightwood-Ashburn Branch.
