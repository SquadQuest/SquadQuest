Fourth Tuesday!

November 24 @ 10:00 am - 5:00 pm
  • « Inside History: A Tour Through the Collections
  • Inside History: A Tour Through the Collections »

Find. Fathom. Feel. Fourth Tuesdays at the Museum!

We are thrilled to introduce Fourth Tuesdays at the Museum! On the fourth Tuesday of every single month, admission to the galleries is completely FREE for everyone, proudly sponsored by Bill Lord in honor of Museum founder Edith Barry.

But that’s not all. Every Fourth Tuesday brings unique experiences to our spaces:

  • Pop-Up History: Get up close with special feature artifacts brought out of the archives just for the day.

  • Activities: Enjoy hands-on activities that connect our local history with contemporary creativity.

  • Fresh Perspectives: See something new hidden in plain sight as you tour our current exhibitions.

Whether you’re a regular visitor or haven’t stepped through our doors in a while, this is the perfect excuse to spend an afternoon immersed in community storytelling.
