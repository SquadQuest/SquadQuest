About Event

​LeadDev SF is back — and we're bringing the energy into 2026. 🎉

​Join us for an evening of lightning talks, good food, and great conversation with a room full of engineers and engineering leaders who are navigating the same rapidly shifting landscape you are.

​Date: Thursday, 16 July 2026 Time: 6 – 9 pm Location: LaunchDarkly, 1999 Harrison St Suite 1100, Oakland, CA 94612

​Speakers to be announced soon!

​Schedule: 6:00 pm – Arrival, networking and refreshments

6:20 pm – Welcome from hosts, Goodness Daramola & Kashyap Tumkur

6:30 pm – Glo Maldonado, Founder & CTO

6:50 pm – Break

7:00 pm – Scarlett Attensil, Developer Educator at LaunchDarkly

7:25 pm – Closing remarks

7:30 pm – Networking

8:30 pm – Close

​Spaces are limited – RSVP to save your spot.

​Questions? Reach us at sanfrancisco-meetups@leaddev.community. Please take a moment to review our Code of Conduct.

Getting There

​For those driving or using public transit, here is some additional information:

  • ​Parking Garage: We have paid for the parking garage to remain open for this event, however, guests will be responsible for parking garage rates

    • ​Standard parking cost is $5.00 every 30 minutes, or a $30 maximum daily rate.

  • ​Street Parking: There is plenty of street parking available at 5:30pm. Street parking is free after 6:00 PM.

  • ​Public Transit: The BART station is just one block away.

​Check-in

​All guests will need to check in at the security desk upon arrival, and they will be sent up to the 11th floor.
