Dice Live in San Francisco

Dice, the indie pop and surf rock sensation from Perth, Australia, brings their distinctive sound to San Francisco. Known for their captivating melodies and vibrant live shows, Dice has built a dedicated following with tracks like "Stop Sign" and "Double Edged Sword." Their performance promises a blend of catchy hooks and laid-back grooves, showcasing why they are a rising force in the alternative music scene. Expect an engaging set from this dynamic band.

Cafe Du Nord Venue Experience

Cafe Du Nord offers an intimate and historic setting for live music in San Francisco. This legendary venue provides an up-close experience, typically featuring general admission and standing room, allowing fans to immerse themselves fully in the performance. Be aware that Cafe Du Nord is a cashless venue, accepting only card payments. It's recommended to arrive early to secure a good viewing spot for Dice's show and to allow time for security screening.
