Sun, Nov 22, 5pm - Mon, Nov 23, 9pm CST

Description

The Youth Evangelism Celebration (YEC) is an evangelistic and equipping event designed to lead young people to Christ and challenge believers to greater accomplishments in Christ. Complete with high energy, inspirational youth speakers; top Christian vocalists and musicians; life changing youth testimonies; and sensational multi-media presentations, this event will erupt with the powerful message of salvation and the dynamic journey of discipleship. The Youth Evangelism Celebration is the greatest place, the greatest “front-door” for bringing lost teenagers and growing believers!

On-Site Check In: Adult contact persons are the only people allowed in the on-site registration/check in area. We ask that no more than two adults per group enter the registration area. You will receive a packet that contains wristbands for your group and other important information.

Wristbands: Each church group registering for the conference will receive one wristband for each registered person. The wristband will be the person’s “ticket” to enter the conference and must be worn on the wrist at all times during the conference.

Seating: Seating is reserved. Seats are reserved on first-come-first-reserved basis as your registration is received here at the Youth Ministry Office. No seats will be reserved for your church until all registration fees have been paid and are on file.

No infants or children 6 years or under. The primary purpose of YEC is to reach teenagers with the gospel and the sessions are carefully designed to be attractive to teenagers (i.e. higher volume, lights, etc.). We want to keep distractions to a minimum. In addition, children also take a seat from a student. Please make other arrangements for your children.
