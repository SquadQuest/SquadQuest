"Almost Maine"

The W&L Department of Theater, Dance and Film Studies Presents: "Almost Maine"

Wednesday-Friday, November 11, 12, 13, 2026 at 7:30 p.m.
Saturday, November 14, 2026 at 2 p.m.
Johnson Theatre/Lenfest Hall/Lenfest Center

Tickets are required. General Public $18; Senior: $16; W&L Faculty/Staff: $14; W&L Student/Student: $8. Taxes and processing fees included in ticket pricing. 

This romantic comedy by John Cariani takes place in the remote town of Almost, Maine, where the town's residents meet and fall in and out of love in unexpected and hilarious ways.

"ALMOST, MAINE" is presented by arrangement with Concord Theatricals on behalf of Dramatists Play Service. www.concordtheatricals.com
